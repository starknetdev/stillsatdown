stillsatdown

Privacy policy

Updated 7 October 2026.

stillsatdown is a private application operated by its owner for personal email organisation. It is not available for public registration.

Information accessed

With the owner's authorisation, the application accesses an authorised Gmail mailbox containing forwarded email copies. It reads message identifiers, senders, recipients, subjects, dates, labels, bounded message text and attachment metadata. It also accesses basic Google account identity information for authentication. Attachment downloads are currently disabled.

How information is used

Information is used to organise email and identify deadlines, renewals, amounts, contacts and reminders for the owner. Initial classification and extraction use a local AI model. Extracted information is available through private tools and may be supplied to AI assistants selected by the owner when the owner requests help with that information. This may involve processing by those service providers under their applicable terms and privacy settings.

The application does not sell email data, use it for advertising or use it to train general purpose AI models. Access and transfer are limited to providing the owner's requested email organisation and assistance features. The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and protection

Mailbox credentials and cached message text are held in a separate protected account on the owner's computer. Other assistant processes receive validated extracts rather than unrestricted access to mailbox credentials or the private message cache. The public homepage and this policy contain no mailbox messages or extracted personal records.

Retention and deletion

Automatic retention cleanup is currently disabled. Cached messages and extracted information remain until the owner removes them. The configured cleanup targets are 30 days for cached message text and 395 days for extracted facts, but these targets are not enforced while cleanup is disabled. The current Gmail integration does not delete mailbox messages.

The owner can request removal of local stored data using the contact address below. Revoking Google access stops future authorised collection but does not automatically delete existing local data or original emails in other mailboxes.

Access controls

The owner can revoke the application's access in the Google Account connections settings. The current integration requests Gmail read access and does not send email or modify messages.

Contact and changes

For questions about the application or stored information, contact digestnews24@gmail.com. This policy will be updated when the application's data handling changes.
