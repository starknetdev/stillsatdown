stillsatdown

A private email assistant operated by its owner for personal use.

The assistant reads an authorised mailbox containing forwarded email copies and organises useful information, including deadlines, renewals, amounts and reminders. Initial email classification uses a local AI model. The resulting information is available to the owner through private tools.

The current mailbox integration requests Gmail read access. It does not send email, modify messages or delete messages. Attachment downloads are currently disabled.

This application is for the owner's personal use and does not offer public registration.
